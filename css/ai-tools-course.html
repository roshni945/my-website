🤖 Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

AI systems can learn from data, recognize patterns, understand language, make decisions and solve problems.

💡 Simple Definition

Artificial Intelligence means teaching computers to think, learn and solve problems like humans.

🧠 Types of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence can be divided into different categories based on its capabilities.

1️⃣ Narrow AI (Weak AI)

Designed for one specific task only.

  • ChatGPT
  • Google Translate
  • Siri
  • Alexa

2️⃣ General AI

A future AI capable of performing almost every intellectual task that a human can do.

3️⃣ Super AI

A theoretical AI that would be smarter than humans in every field. Currently, it does not exist.

⚠ Important

Today's AI tools like ChatGPT and image generators are examples of Narrow AI.

⚡ AI vs Machine Learning vs Deep Learning

Many beginners think Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Deep Learning are the same. However, they are different concepts.

🤖 Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Artificial Intelligence is the broad field of creating machines that can perform tasks requiring human intelligence.

  • Voice Assistants
  • ChatGPT
  • Face Recognition
  • Recommendation Systems

🧠 Machine Learning (ML)

Machine Learning is a subset of AI. It enables computers to learn from data without being explicitly programmed.

  • Email Spam Detection
  • Netflix Recommendations
  • Stock Price Prediction
  • Fraud Detection

🧬 Deep Learning (DL)

Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning. It uses Artificial Neural Networks to solve complex problems.

  • Self Driving Cars
  • Image Recognition
  • Speech Recognition
  • Medical Image Analysis

📊 Comparison Table

Feature AI ML DL
Meaning Artificial Intelligence Machine Learning Deep Learning
Works On Rules + Data Data Large Data
Human Intervention High Medium Low
Complexity Low Medium High
Examples ChatGPT Spam Filter Self Driving Car
💡 Easy Trick to Remember

AI is the biggest field. Machine Learning is inside AI. Deep Learning is inside Machine Learning.

🧠 Large Language Models (LLMs)

Large Language Models are advanced AI systems trained on massive amounts of text. They understand language and generate human-like responses.

Popular LLMs

  • ChatGPT (GPT)
  • Gemini
  • Claude
  • Llama
  • Mistral

⚙️ How LLMs Work

  1. Read billions of words during training.
  2. Learn patterns in language.
  3. Predict the most likely next word.
  4. Generate complete answers.
⚠ Important

LLMs do not "think" like humans. They predict the most likely sequence of words based on patterns learned during training.

🔤 What are Tokens?

A token is a small unit of text processed by an AI model. A word may be one token, or it may be split into multiple tokens depending on the language and text.

Example

Sentence: I love Artificial Intelligence.

This sentence is divided into multiple tokens before the AI processes it.

✨ Prompt Engineering Basics

A prompt is the instruction or question you give to an AI model. Good prompts usually produce better results.

❌ Weak Prompt

Write Python.

✅ Better Prompt

Write a beginner-friendly Python program
to calculate the area of a rectangle.
Explain each line with comments.

📌 Tips for Better Prompts

  • 🎯 Be specific.
  • 📝 Mention the goal clearly.
  • 👨‍🎓 Mention the audience (beginner, student, developer).
  • 📄 Ask for examples if needed.
  • 🔁 Refine the prompt if the first answer isn't what you want.

🚀 Advanced Prompt Engineering

Prompt Engineering is the skill of writing effective instructions that help AI generate accurate, useful and high-quality responses.

1️⃣ Zero-Shot Prompting

Zero-shot prompting means asking AI to perform a task without giving any examples. 

Explain Artificial Intelligence
in simple words.

2️⃣ One-Shot Prompting

One-shot prompting provides one example before asking AI. 

Example:

Input:
HTML

Output:
HyperText Markup Language

Now explain:

CSS

3️⃣ Few-Shot Prompting

Few-shot prompting provides multiple examples to improve AI accuracy. 

HTML → Structure

CSS → Styling

JavaScript → Interactivity

Python → ?

4️⃣ Role Prompting

Tell AI to behave like a specific expert. 

You are an experienced Python teacher.

Explain loops for beginners
with examples.

5️⃣ Chain of Thought Prompting

Ask AI to explain its reasoning step by step. 

Solve this problem step by step
and explain every calculation.

6️⃣ Prompt Template

Part Description
Role Who AI should act as
Task What AI should do
Context Background information
Format Output style
Audience Who will read it
💡 Pro Tip

A good prompt usually includes: Role + Goal + Context + Constraints + Output Format.

⚠ Common Mistake

Very short prompts usually produce generic answers. Add enough context to get better responses.
